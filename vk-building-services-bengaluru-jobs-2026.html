VK Building Services Recruitment 2026

VK Building Services has announced walk-in interview opportunities in Bengaluru for experienced professionals across multiple departments of building services and construction. The recruitment includes senior management, engineering, supervisory, safety, stores and procurement positions. Candidates with relevant qualifications and practical project experience can consider the suitable position according to their skills and previous work background.

The current recruitment covers important technical disciplines including MEP, HVAC, Electrical, Fire Protection System (FPS), Public Health Engineering (PHE), Safety, Material and Stores, and Procurement. These departments are essential for the successful execution of modern building and construction projects. Professionals who have experience in these areas can attend the walk-in interview and discuss suitable opportunities with the recruitment team.

According to the recruitment poster, walk-in interviews are scheduled on 05 September 2026, 11 September 2026, 19 September 2026 and 26 September 2026. The interview timing mentioned in the poster is 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Candidates should select a suitable date and should verify the latest interview status before travelling to the venue.

The interview venue is VK Building Services Pvt. Ltd., #273/3, 3rd Floor, Salarpuria Mona Lisa, 6th Main, 100 ft. Road, HAL II Stage, Indiranagar, Bengaluru – 560038. Candidates should reach the venue with sufficient time and carry all documents mentioned in the recruitment notice.

Current Open Positions

1. MEP Deputy General Manager (DGM)

The MEP Deputy General Manager position is a senior-level opportunity for experienced professionals who have strong exposure to MEP project management and building-services execution. Candidates should be able to demonstrate their previous project responsibilities, technical coordination experience, team management and overall construction exposure. Senior professionals should clearly mention major projects and responsibilities in their updated CV.

2. MEP Assistant General Manager (AGM)

The MEP Assistant General Manager role is suitable for experienced professionals involved in MEP coordination and project execution. Candidates should highlight their experience in managing technical teams, coordinating contractors, monitoring project activities and supporting overall MEP delivery. Applicants should provide accurate details of their previous experience.

3. Project Manager – MEP / HVAC / Electrical / FPS / PHE

Project Manager positions are available for professionals with relevant experience in MEP, HVAC, Electrical, Fire Protection System or PHE. Candidates should mention project type, project size, technical specialization, manpower handled, contractor coordination and major responsibilities in their resume. Strong project execution experience can be highlighted during the interview.

4. HVAC Senior Engineer

The HVAC Senior Engineer position is suitable for experienced HVAC professionals. Candidates with exposure to HVAC installation, site execution, technical coordination, testing and commissioning can apply. The resume should clearly describe previous HVAC projects and actual responsibilities handled by the candidate.

5. HVAC Engineer / Supervisor

HVAC Engineers and Supervisors with practical construction-site experience can attend the interview. Candidates should mention their experience in HVAC installation, supervision, contractor coordination, site activities and other relevant responsibilities. Applicants should apply according to their actual technical experience.

6. Electrical Senior Engineer

The Electrical Senior Engineer opportunity is intended for experienced electrical professionals. Candidates should highlight their building electrical experience, project execution, technical coordination, installation supervision and engineering responsibilities. Previous project experience should be presented clearly in the CV.

7. Electrical Engineer / Supervisor

Electrical Engineers and Supervisors with relevant construction and building-services experience can apply. Candidates should mention their electrical installation, site supervision, coordination and project responsibilities. A clear resume will help the recruitment team understand the candidate's experience.

8. FPS Engineer

FPS refers to Fire Protection System. Candidates with experience in fire-fighting and fire-protection systems can apply for this position. Relevant experience in installation, testing, commissioning, inspection and coordination with other building-services teams should be included in the candidate's CV where applicable.

9. PHE Engineer

Public Health Engineering professionals can apply for the PHE Engineer position. Candidates with experience in plumbing, water supply, drainage, sanitary systems and other public-health services should clearly mention their project experience. Plumbing and PHE professionals should explain their site responsibilities and coordination experience during the interview.

10. Safety Manager

The Safety Manager position is suitable for experienced construction safety professionals. Candidates should highlight experience in site safety management, inspections, hazard identification, safety compliance, toolbox meetings, safety monitoring and coordination with project teams. Relevant safety certifications should also be mentioned in the resume.

11. Safety Engineer / Supervisor

Safety Engineers and Supervisors with relevant construction experience can attend the walk-in interview. Candidates should describe their previous safety responsibilities, site inspections, safety reporting, worker awareness activities and compliance-related experience according to their actual background.

12. Material Officer / Store Keeper

Material Officers and Store Keepers can apply for the listed position. Candidates with experience in construction material receiving, stock control, inventory management, material issuing, documentation and coordination with project teams should highlight these skills in their resume. Accuracy and proper material records are important aspects of construction-store operations.

13. HVAC / PHE / Electrical Procurement

Procurement professionals with experience in HVAC, PHE or Electrical materials can apply. Candidates should mention experience in vendor coordination, material sourcing, quotations, purchase processes, supplier communication and project-material requirements. Knowledge of construction materials and procurement procedures can be useful for this role.

How to Prepare for the Walk-In Interview

Candidates should prepare an updated and professional resume before attending the interview. The CV should clearly mention the candidate's current designation, total years of experience, previous companies, project locations, educational qualification and technical skills. Candidates should avoid adding incorrect information and should provide only genuine experience details.

MEP, HVAC, Electrical, FPS and PHE candidates should mention the type of projects they have worked on and their actual responsibilities. For example, candidates can highlight site execution, installation, testing, commissioning, contractor coordination, team supervision and project documentation when these activities are part of their real experience.

Safety professionals should keep their safety certifications and experience information ready. Store and procurement professionals should be prepared to explain their experience in material management, inventory, vendor coordination, purchase activities and documentation. Senior-level candidates should also be ready to discuss team management, project coordination and previous project responsibilities.

Candidates attending a walk-in interview should reach the venue with sufficient time. It is better to carry both the updated resume and important supporting documents. Candidates should keep their phone number and email address active because the recruitment team may use these details for further communication regarding the selection process.

Why Building Services Jobs Are Important

Building-services projects require coordination between many technical departments. MEP, HVAC, Electrical, Fire Protection, PHE, Safety, Stores and Procurement teams work together to support successful project execution. Each department has a specific responsibility and requires trained and experienced professionals.

For professionals working in the construction and building-services industry, suitable project opportunities can provide exposure to different technical systems and multidisciplinary coordination. Engineers and supervisors can develop their knowledge by working with project teams, contractors, consultants and other technical departments.

Candidates should select a position based on their actual qualification and experience. Applying for a position that matches the candidate's background can make the interview process more useful and can help the recruitment team evaluate the candidate correctly.
