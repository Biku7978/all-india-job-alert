🏢 About Tata Consulting Engineers

Tata Consulting Engineers (TCE) is one of India's leading engineering consulting organizations and a proud member of the Tata Group. The company has successfully completed numerous Power, Infrastructure, Industrial, Water, Transportation and EPC projects in India as well as overseas. TCE is widely recognized for delivering high-quality engineering solutions while maintaining international standards in safety, quality and project execution.

Working with Tata Consulting Engineers provides excellent exposure to large infrastructure developments, modern engineering technologies, experienced professionals and long-term career growth. Employees receive opportunities to enhance their technical knowledge while working on prestigious national and international projects.

💼 Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will be responsible for planning, execution, monitoring and successful completion of assigned engineering projects. Responsibilities include project planning, quality assurance, safety management, contractor coordination, documentation, client communication, progress monitoring and timely completion of work as per company standards.

Depending on the role, engineers may supervise site activities, prepare project reports, monitor manpower, coordinate procurement activities and ensure compliance with safety and quality guidelines. Strong technical knowledge and teamwork skills will be an added advantage.
