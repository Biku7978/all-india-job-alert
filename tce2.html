📢 Latest TCE Recruitment 2026

Tata Consulting Engineers (TCE), a leading engineering consultancy company under the Tata Group, has announced a new recruitment drive for experienced professionals. 🎓 Qualification

Candidates with B.E., B.Tech, Diploma or other relevant qualifications in Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Instrumentation or related engineering disciplines are eligible to apply, depending on the post and experience requirements.

Selected candidates will be responsible for planning, execution, monitoring, quality control, safety management, project coordination, billing, documentation, procurement support and successful completion of engineering projects as per company standards. Depending on the position, employees may also coordinate with clients, consultants, contractors and site teams to ensure timely project delivery.

Salary will be offered according to the candidate's qualification, experience and interview performance. Along with an attractive salary package, the company provides career growth opportunities, exposure to major infrastructure projects, professional learning, and a safe working environment. Other company benefits will be as per Tata Consulting Engineers policies.

Interested and eligible candidates should attend the walk-in interview with an updated resume, passport-size photographs, educational certificates, experience certificates, salary slips (if available), Aadhaar Card/PAN Card and other relevant documents.
