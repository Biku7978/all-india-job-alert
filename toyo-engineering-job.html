3. Structural Steel Engineer

Candidates with structural steel construction experience may be considered. Knowledge of structural erection, fabrication, inspection, drawings, measurements and site coordination can be valuable for this position.

4. Piping Site Engineer

Piping professionals with 4–6 years of experience may apply. Relevant exposure to industrial piping construction, installation, testing, inspection, drawings, supports and site execution is useful.

5. Mechanical QA/QC Engineer

Mechanical QA/QC professionals with experience in industrial construction projects can apply. Knowledge of inspection, documentation, quality procedures and coordination with construction and engineering teams may be required.

6. Mechanical Site Engineer

Mechanical Site Engineer candidates with relevant industrial construction experience are invited to apply. Candidates should preferably have hands-on exposure to site execution and coordination.
