1. Safety Officer – Chennai

Aditya Infrastructure Pvt Ltd is looking for experienced Safety Officers for its Chennai location. The required experience for this position is 5 to 7 years. Candidates should have a NEBOSH qualification or Diploma in Safety.

This opportunity is suitable for safety professionals who have practical experience in construction, infrastructure or project environments. Candidates should have a good understanding of workplace safety procedures, site safety monitoring and coordination with project teams.

Applicants should clearly mention their safety experience and relevant qualification in their updated CV. Candidates with relevant industry experience and immediate joining availability are encouraged to share their profile.

2. Site Engineer – Chennai

The Site Engineer position requires 5 to 7 years of experience. Candidates should have B.E, B.Tech or DCE qualification. The vacancy is based in Chennai and is suitable for engineering professionals with practical site and project experience.

Candidates should mention their previous project experience, site responsibilities, technical skills and total years of experience in their CV. Professionals who have worked in infrastructure or construction projects can consider applying for this position.

Immediate joiners are preferred according to the recruitment information. Candidates should therefore mention their joining availability clearly when sharing their CV with the recruitment team.

3. Shift In-Charge – Chennai

The Shift In-Charge position requires 3 to 5 years of experience and Any Degree is mentioned as the qualification. The role is suitable for professionals who have experience managing shifts, coordinating manpower and monitoring daily operational or site activities.

Candidates should have good communication and coordination abilities. Relevant infrastructure, construction or operational experience can be helpful. Applicants should clearly describe their previous shift responsibilities and management experience in their updated CV.

4. QC In-Charge – Chennai

Aditya Infrastructure is also hiring QC In-Charge candidates with 3 to 5 years of experience. Any Degree is mentioned as the qualification for this position.

Candidates with relevant quality-control experience can apply. Applicants should mention their previous quality-related responsibilities, project experience, inspections and coordination work in their CV.

Professionals who have practical experience in quality control and construction or infrastructure projects should consider this opportunity. The position is based in Chennai.
