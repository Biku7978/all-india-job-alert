📢 Delta Group Recruitment 2026

Delta Group has announced a new recruitment drive for experienced professionals in Railway, Building, Oil & Gas and Refinery Infrastructure projects across India. The company is inviting applications from qualified candidates for multiple engineering, QA/QC, HSE, HR, Billing and Field Management positions.

This recruitment offers an excellent opportunity for professionals who want to build a long-term career with a reputed infrastructure company. Selected candidates will get the opportunity to work on large-scale projects while enhancing their technical and management skills.

📋 Available Positions

  • HSE Manager
  • QA/QC (Mechanical) Manager
  • QA/QC Senior Engineer
  • Field Management Senior Supervisor
  • Surveyor
  • Human Resources Senior Executive
  • HSE Supervisor
  • Senior Engineer Billing

🎓 Educational Qualification

Candidates with B.E., B.Tech, Diploma, Graduation or Post Graduation in relevant disciplines are eligible to apply based on the position. Applicants with experience in Railway, Building, Oil & Gas, Refinery or Infrastructure projects will be given preference. Candidates must possess strong technical knowledge, communication skills and the ability to work in a professional project environment.

💼 Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will be responsible for project execution, quality assurance, safety implementation, field supervision, project planning, billing, documentation, survey work and team coordination. Engineers and supervisors will ensure that work is completed according to company standards, safety guidelines and project schedules. QA/QC professionals will monitor quality standards, HSE professionals will maintain workplace safety, while HR executives will support recruitment and employee management activities.

💰 Salary & Benefits

Salary will depend on the candidate's qualifications, experience and interview performance. Delta Group offers a competitive salary package, career growth opportunities, exposure to large infrastructure projects, professional learning, and other company benefits as per organizational policy. Employees will also get the opportunity to work with experienced professionals on prestigious projects across India.
