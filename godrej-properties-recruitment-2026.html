🎓 Educational Qualification

For supervisory and foreman positions, candidates with a relevant technical background and strong practical construction experience may be suitable. Practical knowledge of site execution, manpower handling, drawings, measurements, construction materials, quality requirements and safety procedures can be important for these roles.

💼 RCC Engineer – Job Role

The RCC Engineer position is suitable for experienced civil engineering professionals who have practical knowledge of reinforced cement concrete construction. The selected candidate may be responsible for monitoring RCC activities, checking reinforcement arrangements, coordinating with site teams, reviewing drawings and ensuring that construction work follows approved specifications.

The engineer may also coordinate with contractors, supervisors and other departments during slab, beam, column, foundation and other structural activities. Candidates should have good knowledge of construction practices and should be capable of identifying site issues and coordinating with the relevant team for timely resolution.

⚙️ MEP Engineer – Job Role

The MEP Engineer position involves coordination and supervision of mechanical, electrical and plumbing-related services within construction projects. Candidates should understand MEP drawings, site coordination, installation activities, inspection procedures and project requirements.

The selected professional may coordinate with civil and finishing teams to ensure that MEP services are installed at the correct stages of construction. Good communication, technical understanding and the ability to coordinate with contractors and project teams can be important for this position.

🏗️ Infra Engineer – Job Role

The Infra Engineer may be responsible for infrastructure-related construction activities and site coordination. The role can involve monitoring site execution, checking work progress, coordinating with contractors and ensuring that construction activities are completed according to project requirements.

Candidates should have practical knowledge of construction processes and should be comfortable working at project sites. Experience with infrastructure projects, site documentation, measurements and contractor coordination can be beneficial.

👷 Shuttering Foreman – Job Role

The Shuttering Foreman is responsible for supervising shuttering and formwork activities at the construction site. Candidates should have strong practical experience in formwork, manpower management and site execution.

The selected candidate may coordinate with workers and supervisors, check shuttering arrangements and ensure that the work is completed according to drawings and project requirements. Safety and quality requirements should also be followed during execution.

🧱 Concrete Supervisor – Job Role

The Concrete Supervisor is responsible for supervising concrete-related activities at the project site. The position may involve coordinating concrete placement, checking site readiness, managing workers and communicating with engineers and other project teams.

Candidates should understand basic concrete construction practices and should have experience handling site activities. Knowledge of concrete quality, pouring procedures, manpower coordination and construction safety can be useful for this role.

🏠 Finishing Engineer – Job Role

The Finishing Engineer may be responsible for monitoring finishing activities such as plastering, flooring, painting, doors and windows, false ceiling, waterproofing and other finishing works. The selected candidate should coordinate with contractors and site teams to ensure that finishing work is completed according to project quality requirements.

Attention to detail is important for finishing activities because the final appearance and quality of a building depend heavily on proper finishing execution. Candidates should be capable of identifying defects and coordinating corrective work.

🦺 Safety Executive – Job Role

The Safety Executive is responsible for supporting workplace safety at construction sites. The role may include monitoring safety practices, conducting site inspections, identifying unsafe conditions and communicating safety requirements to workers and contractors.

Candidates should understand basic construction safety procedures and should be capable of communicating safety instructions clearly. Experience in construction safety and knowledge of personal protective equipment, work-at-height safety and site safety procedures can be valuable.
