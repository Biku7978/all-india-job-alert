🏗️ RCC Engineer

The RCC Engineer position is suitable for professionals experienced in reinforced cement concrete construction and structural execution. The selected candidate may monitor RCC activities at the project site, check reinforcement and shuttering work, coordinate with contractors and ensure that construction activities are completed according to approved drawings and project requirements.

Candidates should have practical knowledge of reinforcement detailing, shuttering, concreting, quantity checking, site coordination and construction quality procedures. Experience in high-rise residential or commercial construction can be useful for this position.

⚙️ MEP Engineer

The MEP Engineer position is suitable for professionals experienced in Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing services associated with building construction. MEP professionals are generally involved in coordination between different services and civil construction activities.

Responsibilities may include checking MEP installations, coordinating with contractors, reviewing drawings, monitoring site progress, resolving technical issues and ensuring that work is completed according to approved specifications. Candidates should have good coordination skills and practical site experience.

🏗️ Infra Engineer

The Infra Engineer role is suitable for candidates with experience in infrastructure-related construction activities. Depending on project requirements, responsibilities may include site supervision, execution monitoring, contractor coordination, quality checking, measurements, documentation and progress reporting.

Candidates with experience in large-scale construction and infrastructure projects may be preferred. The ability to understand drawings, coordinate with project teams and maintain construction schedules is important for this type of position.

👷 Shuttering Foreman

The Shuttering Foreman position is suitable for experienced construction professionals who have practical knowledge of formwork and shuttering activities. The selected candidate may supervise shuttering teams, coordinate manpower, check formwork alignment and dimensions and ensure that shuttering work is completed safely and according to project requirements.

Experience with high-rise building construction, columns, beams, slabs, walls and other RCC elements can be valuable. Candidates should have strong site supervision skills and the ability to manage workers and subcontractors.

🏢 Concrete Supervisor

The Concrete Supervisor role involves supervising concrete-related activities at the construction site. Responsibilities can include coordinating concrete pouring, checking manpower, monitoring work progress, coordinating with batching plants and ensuring that concreting activities are completed according to project procedures.

Candidates should understand basic concrete construction practices, site safety requirements, quality procedures and coordination between different construction teams. Previous experience in high-rise construction can be beneficial.

🎨 Finishing Engineer

The Finishing Engineer position is suitable for professionals experienced in finishing activities in residential, commercial or high-rise buildings. Finishing work may include plastering, flooring, tiling, painting, doors, windows, false ceilings and other architectural finishing activities.

The selected professional may coordinate with contractors, monitor work quality, check progress, resolve site issues and ensure that finishing activities are completed according to project specifications and schedules.

🦺 Safety Executive

The Safety Executive position is intended for professionals who have experience in construction safety and workplace safety management. The role can involve monitoring safety practices, conducting site inspections, identifying unsafe conditions, maintaining safety records and supporting implementation of safety procedures.

Candidates should have good knowledge of construction-site safety practices and should be capable of communicating safety requirements to workers, supervisors and contractors. Experience in large construction projects can be an advantage.

🎓 Educational Qualification

Educational requirements may vary according to the position. Engineering positions such as RCC Engineer, MEP Engineer, Infra Engineer and Finishing Engineer generally require an appropriate engineering qualification such as B.E. or B.Tech in the relevant discipline. Candidates should verify the exact qualification requirement before applying.

For supervisor and foreman positions, practical construction experience and relevant technical knowledge are particularly important. Safety professionals should have appropriate safety-related qualifications and experience suitable for construction-site responsibilities.

  • Good understanding of construction activities.
  • Ability to read and understand construction drawings.
  • Strong site coordination skills.
  • Knowledge of construction quality standards.
  • Ability to coordinate with contractors and subcontractors.
  • Good communication and teamwork skills.
  • Ability to monitor project progress.
  • Knowledge of workplace safety requirements.
  • Good documentation and reporting skills.
  • Ability to work according to project schedules.

📌 Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will perform duties according to their respective positions. Engineers may monitor site execution, review drawings, coordinate with contractors, prepare reports and ensure that construction work follows approved specifications.

Supervisors and foremen may manage workers, monitor daily activities, coordinate manpower and ensure that assigned construction work is completed on time. Safety professionals may monitor workplace safety and support implementation of safety procedures.

Employees may also attend project meetings, maintain documentation, report progress to senior management and coordinate with other departments to resolve project-related issues.
