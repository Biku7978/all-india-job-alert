Procon India Pvt. Ltd. Recruitment 2026 – Management Trainee Design Office | Freshers Engineering Jobs

Procon India Pvt. Ltd. has announced an exciting career opportunity for young and talented engineering graduates who are looking to begin their professional journey in Design Engineering. The company is inviting applications for the position of Management Trainee – Design Office at Greater Noida.

This recruitment is suitable for engineering graduates and postgraduates from Mechanical, Civil, Electrical and Instrumentation disciplines. Candidates who have a strong interest in engineering design and want to develop their technical career in an engineering and consultancy environment can consider this opportunity.
