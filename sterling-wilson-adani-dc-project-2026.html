🏗️ About the Project

The recruitment is for a Data Center project in the Shad Nagar area of Hyderabad. Data Center projects require specialized building services and coordinated execution because several technical systems need to operate together. These include electrical infrastructure, HVAC systems, fire protection, plumbing, public health engineering and ELV systems.

Supervisors working on such projects are generally responsible for monitoring daily site activities, coordinating manpower, checking installation work, communicating with engineers and contractors, maintaining work quality and ensuring that activities progress according to the project schedule.

Candidates who have previously worked on commercial buildings, industrial projects, high-rise buildings, hospitals, data centers, infrastructure projects or other large MEP construction projects may find their experience relevant to these vacancies.

🔧 PHE Supervisor

The PHE Supervisor position is suitable for professionals experienced in plumbing and Public Health Engineering services. The selected candidate may supervise plumbing installation activities, coordinate workers and subcontractors, check materials and monitor the progress of PHE works at the project site.

Candidates should have practical knowledge of plumbing systems, drainage, water supply systems, sanitary installations, testing and commissioning activities and construction drawings. Good coordination with civil and other MEP teams is important because plumbing services often need to be installed alongside structural and architectural works.

❄️ HVAC Supervisor

The HVAC Supervisor position is intended for professionals with practical experience in heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems. HVAC is an important part of a Data Center project because temperature control, ventilation and equipment cooling are critical requirements.

The supervisor may monitor installation work, coordinate manpower, check ducting and equipment installation, follow approved drawings and coordinate with engineers and contractors. Candidates should have good knowledge of HVAC site activities and should be capable of managing work teams effectively.

⛽ HSD Supervisor

The HSD Supervisor role is suitable for candidates with experience related to High Speed Diesel systems and associated project activities. Depending on project requirements, responsibilities may include supervision of HSD piping, storage-related installations, equipment connections and coordination with other technical services.

Candidates should understand site safety requirements and should be able to coordinate with engineering teams and contractors. Experience in industrial, commercial, power, data center or infrastructure projects can be useful for this position.

🔥 Fire Fighting Supervisor

The Fire Fighting Supervisor position is suitable for experienced professionals who have knowledge of fire protection systems and site installation activities. The selected candidate may supervise installation of fire fighting piping, equipment and related systems while coordinating with project engineers and contractors.

Candidates should understand fire fighting drawings, installation procedures, testing requirements and site safety practices. Experience in large commercial buildings, industrial projects, high-rise buildings or Data Center projects may be advantageous.

📡 ELV Supervisor

The ELV Supervisor position is intended for professionals experienced in Extra Low Voltage systems. ELV services can include different low-voltage communication, security and building-management related systems depending on the project specifications.

The supervisor may coordinate installation teams, monitor work progress, check installation quality, coordinate with other MEP services and ensure that site activities follow approved drawings and project requirements.

⚡ Electrical Supervisor

The Electrical Supervisor position is suitable for candidates with experience in electrical construction and installation activities. Responsibilities may include supervising electrical works, coordinating electricians and subcontractors, checking installations and monitoring work progress.

Candidates should have practical knowledge of electrical site activities and should be comfortable working with engineering teams and project contractors. Experience in commercial buildings, industrial projects, high-rise construction or Data Center projects can be beneficial.

💼 General Job Responsibilities

Selected supervisors will be responsible for managing and monitoring daily site activities related to their respective departments. They may coordinate workers, subcontractors and site engineers to ensure that construction activities are completed according to the project plan.

  • Supervise daily site activities.
  • Coordinate manpower and subcontractors.
  • Read and understand approved drawings.
  • Monitor installation and construction progress.
  • Check workmanship and quality of completed activities.
  • Coordinate with civil, MEP and project teams.
  • Report daily work progress to senior engineers.
  • Maintain proper site documentation where required.
  • Follow project safety procedures.
  • Support testing and commissioning activities.
  • Identify site issues and coordinate with engineers for solutions.
  • Ensure work is completed according to project schedules.
