  • AGM (Civil)
  • Assistant Vice President (AVP – Civil)
  • Senior QA/QC Engineer
  • Senior Billing Engineer

  • Junior QA/QC Engineer
  • Junior Billing Engineer

🎓 Educational Qualification

Applicants should possess B.E. / B.Tech in Civil Engineering or an equivalent qualification from a recognized university. Candidates with relevant experience in High-Rise Residential, Commercial or Industrial Construction Projects will be given preference.

The company expects applicants to have strong technical knowledge, excellent communication skills, teamwork abilities and practical experience in modern construction practices. Candidates must be capable of working under project deadlines while maintaining quality and safety standards.

💼 Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will be responsible for planning, executing and monitoring high-rise building construction activities according to company quality standards and project timelines. Candidates should be capable of coordinating with clients, consultants, subcontractors and project teams while ensuring smooth execution of construction work.

Senior management professionals including AGM (Civil) and AVP (Civil) will supervise complete project execution, manpower management, budgeting, client coordination and project delivery. They will also monitor construction quality, safety compliance and overall project performance.

QA/QC Engineers will inspect construction activities, monitor material quality, prepare inspection reports, maintain QA/QC documentation and ensure that all work complies with approved drawings, IS codes and project specifications.

Planning Engineers will prepare project schedules, monitor work progress, update project reports, coordinate with site teams and ensure timely completion of construction activities using modern planning tools.

Billing Engineers will prepare client bills, subcontractor bills, quantity measurements, BOQ verification and payment documentation while coordinating with project management teams.

Junior Engineers will assist senior engineers in daily site supervision, quality inspections, measurements, documentation and coordination of construction work.

💰 Salary & Benefits

Karan Builders offers competitive salary packages based on qualification, technical knowledge and previous work experience. Apart from attractive salaries, selected candidates will receive professional career growth opportunities, exposure to premium high-rise construction projects, performance incentives, paid leave, training programs and other employee benefits according to company policy.

Employees will work with experienced civil engineering professionals and gain valuable knowledge in project planning, quality management, billing systems and construction management. This provides an excellent opportunity for long-term career growth in India's construction sector.
