L&T Construction Recruitment 2026

L&T Construction has announced an exciting career opportunity for experienced Civil and Structural Design Engineers and Lead Engineers under its Power Transmission & Distribution (PT&D) IC. The recruitment is aimed at professionals who have strong technical knowledge and practical exposure in civil engineering, structural engineering, substation design and large-scale power infrastructure projects.

This opportunity can be suitable for experienced engineering professionals who are looking to work on challenging infrastructure projects and contribute to the design and development of important power transmission and distribution facilities. Candidates with relevant experience in substation design, RCC structures, steel structures, foundations, control-room buildings and multidisciplinary engineering coordination may find this recruitment particularly relevant.

The advertised locations include Chennai, Faridabad and Hyderabad. Depending on project requirements, selected candidates may be assigned to engineering offices or project-related activities. Candidates should carefully review their qualifications and relevant experience before applying.

About the Job Opportunity

The Civil and Structural Design Engineer role involves supporting engineering and design activities associated with large-scale power infrastructure projects. Civil and structural engineers play an important role in the planning, design, development and execution of substations, transmission facilities, distribution infrastructure and associated buildings and structures.

Professionals joining such projects may work with multidisciplinary engineering teams consisting of civil, structural, electrical, mechanical and other technical professionals. Effective coordination between these disciplines is essential for delivering safe, practical and technically compliant infrastructure projects.

The Lead Engineer position may involve additional responsibility for reviewing engineering deliverables, coordinating design teams, managing technical interfaces, supporting project requirements and ensuring that design activities are completed according to applicable standards and project specifications.

Key Areas of Expertise

According to the recruitment information, the required areas of expertise include AIS and GIS substation design, RCC and precast structures, equipment and transformer foundations, steel structures and gantries, cable trenches and equipment foundations, substation roads, grading and drainage, control-room and GIS building design, structural analysis and design, and coordination with electrical and other disciplines.

Candidates with experience in one or more of these areas should clearly highlight their project exposure in their CV. Applicants are encouraged to mention the type of projects handled, their responsibilities, software or engineering tools used, design activities performed and the level of coordination involved.

What L&T Construction Is Looking For

The ideal candidate should have a Civil or Structural Engineering background along with relevant professional experience in substation design, power transmission and distribution projects or similar infrastructure assignments. Strong knowledge of RCC structures, steel structures and foundation design is an important requirement for this type of role.

Candidates should also have an understanding of relevant Indian and international design codes and standards. Practical knowledge of structural design principles, engineering calculations, drawings, technical documentation and multidisciplinary coordination can be valuable for these positions.

The ability to independently manage design deliverables is another important skill. Engineers may need to coordinate with different departments and stakeholders while ensuring that technical information is correctly communicated and project requirements are followed.

Why Civil & Structural Engineers Can Consider This Opportunity

Power infrastructure is an important part of India's growing construction and engineering sector. Substations, transmission networks, distribution systems and associated infrastructure require professionals who understand civil and structural design. Engineers working in this field can gain exposure to complex projects, multidisciplinary coordination and technically demanding infrastructure requirements.

Experienced professionals can also develop their careers by taking responsibility for design packages, technical reviews, coordination activities and project deliverables. Lead-level responsibilities can provide opportunities to develop team-management, technical-review and stakeholder-coordination skills.

Career Growth

A career in civil and structural design for power infrastructure can provide exposure to different project types and engineering challenges. Professionals can build experience in structural design, foundations, substation infrastructure, project coordination, engineering documentation and multidisciplinary interfaces.

With suitable experience and performance, engineers may progress toward senior design, lead engineering, technical coordination and project engineering responsibilities. Continuous learning about engineering standards, design practices, software tools and project management can further strengthen professional development.
