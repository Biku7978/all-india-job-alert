📌 Available Positions

1. AGM

The AGM position is intended for an experienced professional capable of handling important responsibilities within building construction projects. Candidates applying for this level should be prepared to demonstrate leadership, project coordination, planning and team-management experience.

2. Project Manager

The Project Manager role is suitable for professionals experienced in managing construction activities, coordinating teams, monitoring progress, handling site requirements and ensuring that project work is completed according to planned schedules and quality expectations.

3. Billing Engineer

Billing Engineers generally work on measurements, quantities, billing documentation, project records and coordination between site and commercial teams. Candidates with construction billing experience may be considered for this position.

4. Site Engineer

Site Engineers are responsible for supporting day-to-day construction activities, coordinating site teams, monitoring work progress and helping ensure that execution follows project requirements.

5. Quality Engineer (Civil & MEP)

This position is relevant for candidates with quality-control experience in civil or MEP construction work. The role can involve inspection, documentation, quality monitoring and coordination with project teams.

6. Foreman

Foremen are responsible for supervising workers and ensuring that assigned construction activities are carried out properly. Practical site experience, workforce coordination and knowledge of construction work are important for this type of position.

7. Supervisor (Civil & MEP)

Supervisors coordinate field-level construction activities and workers. Candidates with experience in civil or MEP supervision can consider this role if their experience matches the project requirements.

8. Engineer – Plumbing & Fire-Fighting

This position is intended for professionals with experience in plumbing and fire-fighting systems. Candidates should be familiar with site execution, coordination and installation activities related to building services.

9. Surveyor

Surveyors support construction projects through measurement, site surveying and setting-out activities. Candidates with relevant construction surveying experience may be suitable for this position.

🏗️ Project & Work Profile

The recruitment is focused on building construction work. Construction professionals joining such projects may be expected to work with different departments and project teams. Depending on the designation, responsibilities can include site execution, workforce coordination, quality monitoring, billing, engineering support, planning, surveying, MEP coordination, plumbing and fire-fighting activities.

Candidates should understand that construction employment can involve site-based duties, project deadlines, coordination with multiple teams and responsibility for maintaining work quality and safety. Applicants should therefore apply only when their experience and professional background are relevant to the advertised position.

📋 Candidate Preparation

Candidates attending a face-to-face construction interview should keep an updated resume ready. It is recommended to include educational qualifications, total professional experience, current designation, previous project experience, technical skills, current location and contact information in the resume. Candidates should also be prepared to explain their previous responsibilities clearly during the interview.

For engineering and technical positions, applicants may be asked about their practical project experience. Site Engineers may need to explain their experience with construction execution and coordination. Billing Engineers may need to discuss measurement and billing-related work. Quality Engineers may be expected to explain inspection and quality documentation practices. Plumbing and Fire-Fighting Engineers should be ready to discuss relevant building-service installations and site coordination.

For supervisory and management positions, candidates should be prepared to discuss workforce management, project coordination, planning, communication, progress monitoring and handling site-level challenges. Honest information about experience and previous employment should be provided during the recruitment process.
