About Karan Builders Recruitment

The construction and real estate sector offers career opportunities for professionals at different levels. Large residential and commercial projects need project managers, engineers, supervisors, planners, quality professionals and administrative staff to complete projects successfully. Candidates who have practical experience in civil construction and project execution may find the listed positions suitable depending on their qualifications.

Working on construction projects requires technical knowledge as well as the ability to coordinate with multiple teams. Professionals may need to work with contractors, consultants, engineers, supervisors and project management teams. Candidates should therefore mention their previous project experience clearly in their resumes.

Available Job Positions

1. Project Manager – 4 Positions

Candidates with approximately 12–15 years of experience in construction and civil projects may be suitable for this role. B.E. or B.Tech in Civil Engineering is mentioned, and PMP certification may be preferred. Project Managers are generally responsible for project planning, execution, coordination, monitoring and timely completion of construction activities.

2. Junior / Senior Engineer – 6 Positions

Candidates with approximately 3–12 years of experience can apply depending on their level of experience. Civil Engineering qualifications are relevant. Engineers may be responsible for site execution, checking drawings, coordinating with contractors and monitoring construction progress.

3. Site Supervisor – 10 Positions

Candidates with approximately 0–15 years of experience are mentioned for this position. SSC, HSC or relevant Civil qualifications may be considered. Previous site supervision experience can be useful. The role may involve monitoring daily activities, labour coordination and reporting site progress.

4. Quantity Surveyor – 3 Positions

Junior and Senior Quantity Surveyor positions are available. Approximately 5–15 years of experience is mentioned. B.E. or B.Tech Civil candidates with quantity surveying knowledge may be suitable. Experience in measurements, billing and quantity calculations can be beneficial.

5. Quality Lab Technician

Candidates with approximately 1–5 years of experience and relevant Civil Engineering qualifications may apply. Quality laboratory professionals may work on construction material testing, concrete testing, documentation and quality-related records.

6. Planning Engineer – 4 Positions

Candidates with approximately 5–8 years of experience may apply. Civil Engineering qualifications are relevant, and MS Project knowledge may be preferred. Planning Engineers generally assist in preparing schedules, monitoring project progress and supporting project planning activities.

7. Document Controller Engineer – 4 Positions

Candidates with approximately 2–5 years of experience may apply. Civil Engineering or Graduation qualifications are mentioned. Document Controllers are generally responsible for managing project documents, drawings, records and communication-related documentation.

Job Location

Resume Preparation Tips

Before sending your resume, make sure that all information is accurate and updated. Construction companies usually prefer candidates whose resumes clearly explain their practical project experience. Mention the type of projects you have worked on, such as residential buildings, commercial projects, infrastructure projects or industrial construction.

Your CV should also include your current designation, total experience, technical skills, educational qualifications and current location. If you have experience in planning, billing, quality control, site execution or project management, clearly mention these skills.

Selection Process

The selection process may depend on the requirements of the position. Normally, candidates may go through resume screening followed by an HR discussion and technical interview. Shortlisted candidates may also be asked about their previous projects, responsibilities, technical knowledge and current salary expectations.

Applicants should keep their educational certificates, experience documents, identity proof and updated resume ready. Final selection will depend on the recruitment process and the company's eligibility requirements.
