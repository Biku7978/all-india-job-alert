About Afcons Infrastructure Job Opportunity

Afcons Infrastructure Ltd. is looking for experienced professionals for project operations connected with the BMC Water Tunnel Project in Mumbai. Tunnel and underground infrastructure projects require skilled manpower because the work environment involves specialised machinery, mechanical equipment, electrical systems and strict safety procedures.

Candidates with relevant experience in tunnelling, TBM operations, mechanical maintenance, electrical maintenance, locomotive operations, shotcrete work and other infrastructure activities may find this opportunity suitable. Applicants should apply only for positions that match their actual qualifications and work experience.

Technical infrastructure projects require proper teamwork between operators, mechanics, electricians, supervisors and other professionals. Employees working on such projects must follow project procedures, safety requirements and instructions from supervisors. Candidates with experience in large infrastructure or construction projects may have an advantage.

Operator Positions

Operator positions are available for Topbelt, Chilling Plant, Locomotive, Shotcrete and Rail Switch operations. Operators are generally responsible for operating and monitoring assigned machinery or equipment according to approved project procedures. Candidates should understand machine operations and follow all safety requirements.

Tunnel projects often use specialised machinery. Therefore, candidates applying for operator roles should have practical experience related to their specific equipment. Applicants should mention the type of machinery they have operated in their updated CV.

Welder and Mechanical Positions

The company is also hiring candidates for Welder – TBM, Mechanic – Hydraulic, Mechanic – Locomotive, Assistant Mechanic – TBM and Foreman – Mechanical positions. These roles require technical knowledge and practical experience related to mechanical systems and equipment.

Mechanical professionals play an important role in maintaining project machinery. Regular inspection and maintenance help reduce equipment downtime and support smooth project operations. Candidates should mention their technical experience, previous projects and important machinery skills while applying.

Electrical Positions

Electrician positions are available for TBM-related operations. Electrical professionals are responsible for supporting electrical equipment and systems according to approved project procedures. Candidates should have relevant electrical experience and knowledge of safety procedures.

Electrical work on infrastructure projects requires careful attention to safety. Applicants should mention their electrical qualifications, licences or certifications, if applicable, along with their previous experience.
