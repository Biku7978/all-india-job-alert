Project Exposure

Construction Manager

Construction Managers are responsible for supervising and coordinating construction activities at project sites. The position generally requires strong leadership, project coordination and construction management capabilities.

Responsibilities can include monitoring project progress, coordinating site teams, managing contractors and subcontractors, checking resources, coordinating materials and equipment and ensuring that construction activities follow approved drawings and project specifications.

Civil Site Engineer

Civil Site Engineers play an important role in day-to-day construction execution. Their responsibilities may include supervising civil works, checking drawings, coordinating with contractors, monitoring quantities, maintaining site records and ensuring that work is completed according to required quality standards.

Candidates with practical experience in industrial construction, infrastructure projects, RCC works, foundations, piling or structural construction may be suitable for this position.

Senior Civil Engineer

Senior Civil Engineers may be responsible for handling important construction activities and guiding junior engineers and site teams. The role requires practical knowledge of civil construction, technical coordination and problem-solving.

Senior professionals may also coordinate with planning, quality, safety, commercial and project management teams to maintain smooth project execution.

QA/QC Engineer – Civil

QA/QC Engineers are responsible for supporting quality assurance and quality control activities on construction projects. Their work can include inspections, testing coordination, documentation, material verification, quality records and monitoring construction activities against approved specifications.

Candidates with relevant civil quality control experience and knowledge of construction quality procedures can consider applying for this role.

EHS / Safety Engineer

EHS and Safety Engineers help maintain safe working conditions at construction and industrial project sites. Their responsibilities can include safety inspections, toolbox meetings, identifying hazards, maintaining safety records and coordinating corrective actions.

Candidates with construction safety, EHS or industrial safety experience may be considered for safety-related opportunities.

Planning Engineer

Planning Engineers are responsible for supporting project scheduling, progress monitoring and reporting. They may prepare schedules, monitor planned versus actual progress, coordinate with site teams and prepare regular project reports.

Candidates with experience in construction planning, scheduling, progress monitoring and project coordination may be suitable for the Planning Engineer position.

Billing / Quantity Engineer

Billing and Quantity Engineers support commercial and quantity-related activities on construction projects. Their responsibilities can include quantity take-offs, measurements, bill preparation, quantity verification and coordination with project and subcontractor teams.

Candidates with experience in construction billing, quantity surveying, BOQ preparation and measurement activities may find this opportunity relevant.

Survey Engineer

Survey Engineers support construction projects through surveying, setting-out, alignment, level checking and measurement activities. Practical experience in construction surveying can be valuable for this position.
