🏗️ About the Junior RMC Engineer Role

A Junior RMC Engineer plays an important role in ensuring that concrete production and delivery meet project requirements. The professional may be involved in coordinating concrete requirements, checking mix specifications, monitoring quality and communicating with the batching plant and construction teams.

The role can involve monitoring concrete production, checking materials, coordinating concrete dispatches and supporting quality-control activities. The engineer may also need to communicate with site teams regarding concrete quantity, grade, pouring schedules and technical requirements.

Because the position is associated with high-rise residential construction, practical experience in handling RMC activities at large construction projects can be particularly useful.

👷 Job Responsibilities

The selected Junior RMC Engineer may be responsible for monitoring and coordinating Ready Mix Concrete activities at the project site. This can include communicating daily concrete requirements to the relevant teams and ensuring that concrete delivery schedules are coordinated with construction activities.

The engineer may also monitor concrete quality, coordinate with batching plant personnel and support quality-control teams during concrete production and placement. Proper documentation of concrete-related activities may also be part of the job.

Other responsibilities can include checking concrete requirements, coordinating with site engineers, monitoring pouring schedules and supporting the project team in resolving technical issues related to concrete work.

The selected candidate may also need to coordinate with contractors, quality-control engineers and other departments to ensure smooth execution of concrete-related activities.

🏢 High-Rise Residential Project Experience

High-rise residential construction requires careful coordination of concrete production, transportation and placement. RMC Engineers need to understand project schedules and ensure that concrete is available when required by the site team.

Candidates with previous experience in high-rise projects may be familiar with large-volume concrete requirements, continuous pouring operations, coordination with multiple teams and strict quality-control procedures.

🧪 QA/QC & Concrete Quality

Quality control is an important part of Ready Mix Concrete operations. The engineer may work with QA/QC teams to ensure that concrete meets the required project specifications.

Candidates should understand basic concrete testing procedures, material quality, mix requirements and relevant Indian Standards. They should also be able to coordinate with laboratory and site quality teams whenever required.
