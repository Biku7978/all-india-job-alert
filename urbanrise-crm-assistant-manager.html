📌 About the Job

Urbanrise is offering an opportunity for an experienced CRM professional for the position of CRM – Assistant Manager in Kotturpuram, Chennai. The role is particularly suitable for candidates who have experience in the real estate industry, residential projects and post-sales customer relationship management.

The selected professional will be responsible for managing customer relationships after booking and ensuring that the post-sales process is handled efficiently. The position involves regular coordination with customers as well as internal teams such as Sales, Legal, Accounts and Project teams.

Candidates who have practical knowledge of customer coordination, payment follow-up, registration, documentation, property handover and post-sales CRM activities may find this opportunity relevant to their experience.

🧑‍💼 Job Responsibilities

1. Post-Sales CRM Management

The CRM Assistant Manager will act as an important point of coordination for customers after booking. The professional is expected to maintain proper communication with customers and make sure that important post-sales activities are followed up within the required timelines.

2. Customer Data Management

Maintaining accurate customer information is an important part of CRM operations. The role may involve maintaining customer records, agreements, payment information, registration details, handover status and other relevant documentation.

3. Payment Follow-Up

The candidate will be involved in coordinating payment follow-ups with customers. Timely communication and proper tracking of pending payments can help ensure that the post-sales process continues smoothly.

4. Registration & Documentation

The position also involves supporting customers during registration and documentation activities. Candidates with experience handling documents related to property transactions, sale deeds, NOCs and similar processes may be preferred.

5. Internal Team Coordination

The CRM team needs to coordinate with several departments. The Assistant Manager may work closely with Sales, Legal, Accounts and Project teams to resolve customer requirements and keep the post-sales process on track.

6. Customer Queries & Complaints

Customer satisfaction is an important part of CRM. The candidate should be capable of understanding customer concerns, coordinating with the appropriate department and providing timely updates.

7. Site Visits & Property Handover

Customer coordination may also include arranging site visits, providing project updates and assisting customers during property handover activities. Good communication and coordination skills are therefore important for this role.
