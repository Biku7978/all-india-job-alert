💼 Position: CRM – Assistant Manager

The position requires an organised approach because several customer cases can be active at the same time. Maintaining proper records and providing timely updates can help improve customer experience and reduce delays in the post-sales process.

🧑‍💼 Main Job Responsibilities

1. Post-Sales CRM Management

The candidate will act as an important point of coordination for customers after booking. This includes maintaining communication, tracking pending activities and ensuring that customer requirements are passed to the appropriate internal team.

2. Customer Data Management

The role may involve maintaining accurate customer information, booking details, agreements, payment status, registration status and handover information. Proper documentation is important for smooth CRM operations.

3. Payment Follow-Up

Timely payment follow-up is an important responsibility in post-sales operations. The candidate may need to communicate with customers regarding pending payments and coordinate internally to keep payment-related activities updated.

4. Registration & Documentation

The professional may support customers during registration and documentation processes. Knowledge of documents such as sale agreements, sale deeds, NOCs and other property-related paperwork can be useful for this role.

5. Coordination With Internal Departments

The CRM department works closely with Sales, Legal, Accounts and Project teams. The Assistant Manager should be capable of coordinating with these teams and following up until customer issues or requests are resolved.

6. Customer Queries & Complaints

Customer queries should be handled professionally and within a reasonable time. The candidate may need to understand customer concerns, coordinate with the responsible department and provide appropriate updates.

7. Site Visit Coordination

Customer coordination can include arranging or supporting site visits, communicating project updates and helping customers understand the progress of their property.

8. Property Handover Coordination

The CRM team can also be involved in property handover activities. The candidate may coordinate with customers and project teams to make sure that relevant information and documentation are available during the handover process.

🏢 Skills That Can Help in This Role

Candidates applying for this position should have good verbal and written communication skills. Since CRM professionals regularly interact with customers, the ability to explain information clearly and professionally is important.

Time management and follow-up skills are also useful. A CRM professional may handle multiple customers simultaneously, so keeping track of payment status, registration, documentation, customer requests and handover activities can be an important part of daily work.

Knowledge of MS Office, customer databases, CRM software or other business communication tools can also be beneficial, depending on the company's internal processes.
