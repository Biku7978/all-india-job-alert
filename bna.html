📢 BNA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. Recruitment 2026

BNA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. has announced a new recruitment drive for experienced and talented professionals in the civil infrastructure sector. The company is inviting applications from qualified candidates for multiple positions including Manager – HR & Admin, Senior Engineer – Structure, Senior Surveyor, Billing & Planning Manager, RCC Designer, QA/QC Engineer, Planning Engineer, Document Controller, Site Engineer and Office Boy.

This recruitment offers an excellent opportunity for candidates who are looking for long-term career growth in the infrastructure and construction industry. BNA Infrastructure is known for executing high-quality civil engineering projects and provides employees with a professional working environment, attractive salary packages, career development opportunities and exposure to large-scale infrastructure works.

Candidates with B.E., B.Tech in Civil Engineering, Diploma, Graduation and other relevant qualifications are encouraged to apply according to the eligibility requirements of each position. Experienced professionals with strong technical knowledge, project management skills and a commitment to quality and safety will be given preference during the selection process.

The company is seeking dedicated professionals who can contribute to engineering excellence, project execution, planning, quality control and administrative operations. Candidates selected through this recruitment drive will work alongside experienced engineers and project teams while gaining valuable experience on challenging infrastructure projects.

💼 Job Responsibilities

Selected candidates will be responsible for planning, execution, project coordination, quality control, billing, surveying, documentation, structural engineering, RCC design and overall site management according to company standards.
