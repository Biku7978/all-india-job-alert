🎓 Educational Qualification

Candidates should have a B.Tech or Diploma qualification in Civil Engineering. Applicants should also have relevant professional experience suitable for QA/QC responsibilities in water supply, pipeline or civil construction projects.

💼 Experience Required

Candidates with 3 to 8 years of relevant experience can apply. Experience in water supply works, pipeline projects, civil projects and Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) projects is preferred.

🛠️ Key Skills Required

  • Quality Control and Quality Assurance
  • Pipeline and Water Supply Works
  • Inspection and Testing
  • ITP preparation and implementation
  • Method Statements
  • Quality Checklists
  • QA/QC Documentation
  • Project Reporting
  • Knowledge of IS Codes
  • Knowledge of Project Specifications

👷 Job Responsibilities

The selected QA/QC Engineer may be responsible for monitoring quality standards at project sites and ensuring that construction and water supply activities are completed according to approved specifications and project requirements.

  • Monitoring quality of construction activities.
  • Conducting inspection and testing.
  • Maintaining QA/QC records.
  • Preparing checklists and inspection reports.
  • Following approved ITPs and method statements.
  • Coordinating with project and site teams.
  • Checking work according to drawings and specifications.
  • Maintaining project quality documentation.
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable IS Codes.
