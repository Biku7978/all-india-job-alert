Krishna Builder Recruitment 2026

Krishna Builder is inviting experienced professionals and skilled candidates for various positions related to building construction and upcoming projects. The recruitment drive offers opportunities for candidates with experience ranging from 5 years to more than 25 years, depending on the position.

According to the recruitment information, vacancies are available for senior management professionals, project managers, engineers, foremen, safety professionals, accounts executives and HR/administration personnel. Candidates with relevant construction industry experience are encouraged to apply for the position matching their qualifications and experience.

Candidates who have previous experience in government projects may receive preference. Applicants should therefore clearly mention their project experience, designation, total years of experience and technical skills in their updated CV.

Job Locations

The recruitment poster mentions project opportunities in Odisha, Maharashtra, Delhi/NCR, Gujarat and Assam. The exact work location may depend on the position and project requirement.

Candidates should carefully check the applicable project location before travelling for an interview or joining. Applicants who are comfortable working at project sites and relocating according to project requirements may find these opportunities suitable.

Eligibility & Experience

The vacancies are primarily intended for experienced construction professionals. The required experience varies according to the designation. Senior management and project management positions require extensive industry experience, while engineering, supervisory and office-support positions have comparatively lower experience requirements.

Candidates should possess relevant educational qualifications and professional experience according to the position applied for. Experience in government projects is mentioned as preferred in the recruitment information.

Applicants should make sure that their CV contains accurate information about educational qualifications, previous employers, project experience, designation, total experience, current location, contact information and other relevant professional details.

Why Consider These Construction Job Opportunities?

Construction and infrastructure projects require professionals across several departments, including project management, engineering, quality control, planning, safety, billing, procurement, supervision and administration. The advertised vacancies provide opportunities for experienced professionals to work on upcoming building projects in multiple regions.
