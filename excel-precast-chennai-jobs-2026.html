Job Responsibilities

The selected candidates will be responsible for performing duties according to their respective departments and job positions. Production professionals will be involved in planning and monitoring precast manufacturing activities. Planning Engineers will support project schedules and coordinate activities between different departments.

Quality Engineers and Lab Technicians will be responsible for maintaining quality standards, conducting inspections and monitoring testing activities. Safety professionals will help ensure that workplace safety procedures are followed properly.

Procurement and Stores professionals will manage materials, inventory, purchases and coordination with suppliers. Logistics professionals may support the movement and transportation of precast materials and equipment.

Engineering candidates should have good technical knowledge and relevant industry experience. Candidates with previous experience in Precast, Construction or Manufacturing projects may have an advantage depending on the requirements of the position.

Why Consider a Career in the Precast Industry?

The precast industry provides opportunities for professionals from different engineering and construction backgrounds. As construction technology develops, precast manufacturing has become an important part of modern infrastructure and building projects.

Working in a precast factory can provide experience in production planning, concrete technology, quality control, structural components, logistics, material handling and manufacturing operations. Professionals may also gain valuable experience by working with multidisciplinary teams.
