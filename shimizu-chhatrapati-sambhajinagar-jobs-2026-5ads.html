1. QA/QC Manager

The QA/QC Manager position is suitable for experienced construction professionals who have strong knowledge of quality assurance and quality control procedures. Candidates should be familiar with construction quality standards, inspections, documentation, project coordination and quality management practices.

2. QA/QC Engineer – Civil

The Civil QA/QC Engineer will be responsible for monitoring construction activities and ensuring that civil works are completed according to approved drawings, specifications and quality requirements. Candidates with relevant civil construction and quality-control experience can consider this opportunity.

3. QA/QC Assistant Engineer – Civil

This position is suitable for candidates who have experience supporting civil QA/QC activities. Responsibilities may include inspection support, maintaining quality records, checking materials and coordinating with site teams.

4. Assistant Manager – Mechanical

The Assistant Manager – Mechanical role is intended for professionals with suitable mechanical construction experience. Candidates should have the ability to coordinate mechanical activities, supervise site work and support project execution.

5. Engineer – Mechanical

Mechanical Engineers will be involved in project execution and coordination of mechanical works. Candidates with relevant construction-project experience and knowledge of mechanical systems may apply.

6. QA/QC Engineer – Mechanical & Electrical

Shimizu is looking for QA/QC professionals for Mechanical and Electrical works. Candidates should have relevant technical knowledge and practical experience in inspection, testing, documentation and quality-control activities.

7. CAD Operator – Mechanical & Electrical

The CAD Operator position is suitable for candidates who have experience preparing and modifying technical drawings related to mechanical and electrical works. Knowledge of relevant CAD software and construction drawings will be useful for this role.

8. BIM Modeler – Mechanical & Electrical

The BIM Modeler position is intended for professionals experienced in Building Information Modelling for Mechanical and Electrical services. Candidates with practical BIM experience and knowledge of MEP modelling can consider applying.
