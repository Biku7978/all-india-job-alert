Position-wise Career Opportunities

1. Vice President

The Vice President position is a senior leadership opportunity requiring more than 25 years of professional experience. Candidates applying for this role should have strong leadership, strategic planning, project management and business management experience. There are two positions available.

2. DGM Projects

The DGM Projects position requires more than 20 years of experience. The role is suitable for experienced professionals with extensive exposure to project execution, team management, planning, coordination and project delivery. One position is available.

3. FM Head

Raghava is also looking for an experienced FM Head with more than 20 years of experience. Candidates with strong facilities management, operations, maintenance, team coordination and administration experience may be suitable for this position.

4. Purchase and Contracts

The Purchase and Contracts position requires more than 10 years of experience. Professionals with experience in procurement, vendor management, commercial negotiations, contracts and purchase operations can consider applying.

5. QS & Billing Engineer

There are two openings for QS & Billing Engineers. Candidates should have more than 10 years of relevant experience. Professionals with experience in quantity surveying, billing, measurement, estimation, project documentation and commercial activities can apply.

6. Interior Architect

Two Interior Architect positions are available for professionals with more than 10 years of experience. Candidates with relevant architectural, interior design, planning and project coordination experience can submit their updated resume.

7. Planning Engineer

Two Planning Engineer positions are available. Candidates should have at least 5 years of relevant experience. Planning professionals with knowledge of project schedules, progress monitoring, coordination and reporting may be considered for these openings.

8. MEP Manager

Four positions are available for MEP Managers with more than 5 years of experience. Candidates with relevant MEP coordination, project execution, engineering management and site coordination experience can apply.
