KAMAC Engineering Pvt Ltd Recruitment 2026 – Latest Construction Job Opportunities

KAMAC Engineering Pvt. Ltd. has announced new job opportunities for experienced professionals who are looking to build their career in the construction and infrastructure sector. The company is inviting suitable candidates for multiple positions for its upcoming projects. Candidates with relevant experience in highway construction, mechanical work, material management, site supervision, safety and engineering can apply for the available vacancies.

The recruitment drive includes opportunities for candidates at the head office as well as project locations in Bihar. KAMAC Engineering is looking for skilled and experienced professionals who can contribute effectively to project execution and daily site operations. Candidates with relevant qualifications and practical experience are encouraged to submit their updated CV.

Job Locations: Head Office – Siliguri | Project Sites – Bihar

Management Coordinator Jobs

The Management Coordinator position is suitable for candidates who have strong coordination and communication skills. The selected candidate may be responsible for coordinating between management, project teams, departments and other stakeholders. Candidates should be capable of preparing reports, following up on important activities and ensuring that project-related communication is handled efficiently.

Accounts Manager Jobs

The Accounts Manager will be responsible for managing financial records, accounts-related documentation, payments and other financial activities. Candidates with relevant accounting knowledge and experience in construction or infrastructure organizations may have an advantage. Good knowledge of financial documentation and coordination is important for this position.

Junior Purchase Executive Jobs

The Junior Purchase Executive will assist the purchase department in procurement activities. Responsibilities may include vendor coordination, purchase requests, material follow-up and maintaining purchase records. Candidates with knowledge of construction materials and purchasing processes may be suitable for this position.

Road Manager – Bihar Projects

KAMAC Engineering is looking for experienced Road Managers for its Bihar project locations. Candidates with experience in road and highway construction may be preferred. The Road Manager may be responsible for planning and monitoring site activities, coordinating with engineers, managing project progress and ensuring that work is completed according to the required standards.

Candidates with experience in NHAI, highway construction, road projects or major infrastructure projects may find this opportunity suitable. Good project management and team coordination skills will be important.

Mechanical Manager and Mechanical Engineer

Mechanical professionals play an important role in infrastructure and construction projects. The Mechanical Manager and Mechanical Engineer positions may involve equipment management, maintenance coordination, machinery inspection and supporting project operations.

Candidates with relevant mechanical engineering qualifications and practical experience in construction equipment, heavy machinery or infrastructure projects may apply.

Material Engineer

Material Engineers are responsible for monitoring and managing construction materials used in projects. The role may involve quality checks, material testing, coordination with suppliers and maintaining proper documentation. Experience in construction materials and quality standards can be beneficial.

Junior Highway Engineer

Junior Highway Engineers can support senior engineers in planning, execution, monitoring and documentation activities. Candidates with highway construction experience may have an advantage. The position can provide an excellent opportunity for professionals who want to grow their career in the infrastructure sector.

Safety Supervisor

Safety is an important part of every construction project. The Safety Supervisor may be responsible for monitoring safety practices, ensuring the use of personal protective equipment and helping maintain a safe working environment. Candidates with relevant site safety experience can apply.

Store In-Charge

The Store In-Charge will manage construction materials, stock records, inward and outward material transactions and other store-related activities. Knowledge of inventory management and construction materials may be beneficial for this position.

Foreman and Site Operations

The Foreman position requires practical construction experience and the ability to supervise workers. The selected candidate may be responsible for monitoring daily activities, allocating work and ensuring that site tasks are completed properly.

Data Entry Operator

The Data Entry Operator will support the project or office team by maintaining records and entering important information into computer systems. Candidates with good computer knowledge, typing skills and documentation abilities can consider applying for this position.
